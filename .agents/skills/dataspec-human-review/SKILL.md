---
name: dataspec-human-review
description: Prepare compact human decisions or faithfully apply explicit dispositions to DataSpec revisions. Use for scientific/editorial acceptance and closure review, not automatic technical certification.
---

Inputs: exact candidate revisions, evidence, governing contracts, validator
requirements and actual user dispositions if already supplied. Read
[review semantics](../../../dataspec/v0.2/references/governance.md).

- Group related requirements for usability, retaining target revision and review
  dimension for each item. Show exact text/result, authority, limitation and
  advisory critique; do not ask a reviewer to reread an entire project.
- Offer ACCEPT, ACCEPT WITH EDIT, REJECT and NEEDS INVESTIGATION. For proposed
  edits provide exact replacement text. Advice is not human acceptance.
- If dispositions are absent, present the packet and stop dependent closure.
  If already explicit, apply them without requesting redundant permission.
- Retain the decision source, group, individual disposition, previous revision
  bytes and approved edit reference. An edited group may retain an unchanged
  exclusion; document that distinction rather than changing its proposition.
- Use the actual reviewer convention supplied by the project/user; otherwise the
  least-assumptive valid identity. Never invent independent peer review or dates.
- Revalidate revised dependencies and implementation. Preserve old evidence and
  prospective registration times; current review time is a different event.

Outputs: compact [decision sheet](../../../dataspec/v0.2/templates/review_sheet.md)
or attributed review items and new revisions, with remaining blockers explicit.
Technical validity is not acceptance; acceptance is not universal certification.
New delivery identities are not automatically covered by old human attestations.
