---
name: dataspec-evidence-review
description: Assemble traceable evidence and assess claim support from canonical scientific outputs. Use when registering or reviewing findings; does not replace domain analysis or infer evidence from a document's existence.
---

Inputs: bounded question/plan, authority map, actual commands/outputs, validation
results, scope and uncertainties. Use the
[authority template](../../../dataspec/v0.2/templates/authority_map.md) when
ownership is unclear, then existing domain skills rather than inventing rules.

- Preserve observed/computed results separately from interpretation. Evidence
  needs exact input/code/output identities, result locator and validation limits.
- Treat documentary applicability as a judgment requiring evidence. Byte validity
  or family-level descriptions cannot establish exact-object scientific meaning.
- Create minimal claim propositions with supporting/contradicting evidence and
  scope. Narrative is a consumer, never the authoritative claim record.
- Keep unsupported candidates as candidates, with reasons/evidence and explicit
  narrative exclusion. Do not force a positive result or confirmatory hypothesis.
- Record only demonstrated reproducibility dimensions; local runs cannot silently
  become clean-clone, cross-platform or full-render certification.

Outputs: evidence records, qualified/supported/unsupported claims, declared
uncertainties and exact review needs. Validate references with the version-aware
CLI documented in the [central guide](../../../dataspec/v0.2/README.md).
Stop the claimed analytical conclusion when its domain authority is missing;
a documented exclusion or bounded negative eligibility result is legitimate.
