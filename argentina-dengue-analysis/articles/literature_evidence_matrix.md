# Literature evidence matrix

This matrix supports the companion perspective article. It is an audit tool, not
a meta-analysis or a basis for new effect estimates. “Supports” identifies a
bounded manuscript claim; it never transfers a study result directly to the
certified Argentine surveillance artifacts.

| Key | Reference | Geography / design | Mechanism or context | Bounded manuscript support | Limitation | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `vezzani2004` | Vezzani, Velázquez & Schweigmann (2004) | Buenos Aires; weekly field study | Seasonality, oviposition, breeding sites | Seasonal vector ecology is biologically plausible | Two cemeteries; not dengue transmission measurement | mechanism |
| `vezzani2005` | Vezzani et al. (2005) | Buenos Aires; microhabitat field study | Local habitat suitability | Habitat can structure vector presence | Local and entomological scale | mechanism |
| `demajo2013` | De Majo et al. (2013) | Buenos Aires; field data and model | Temperature, egg mortality, oviposition | Microclimates can generate local heterogeneity | Does not observe the manuscript's surveillance outcome | mechanism |
| `fischer2017` | Fischer et al. (2017) | Buenos Aires; nine warm seasons | Long-term oviposition dynamics | Vector dynamics can change across seasons | Cannot establish current national mechanisms | context |
| `montini2021` | Montini et al. (2021) | Buenos Aires; winter field experiment | Cold fronts and immature mortality | Thermal constraints affect vector persistence | One life stage and locality | mechanism |
| `rubio2020` | Rubio et al. (2020) | South America; distribution records | Southern expansion | Range limits are ecologically dynamic | Distribution records are not transmission evidence | context |
| `benitez2021` | Benitez et al. (2021) | Córdoba; ovitrap time series | Weather, vegetation, oviposition | Environmental associations may be lagged and context-specific | Vector eggs, not published counts | mechanism |
| `estallo2015` | Estallo et al. (2015) | Orán; ovitrap time series | Weather and oviposition | Meteorological variables can precede vector activity | Single city and model specification | mechanism |
| `espinosa2016` | Espinosa et al. (2016) | Tartagal; surveillance/control data | Breeding-site spatial heterogeneity | Urban habitat can structure vector habitat | Entomological, not case-level study | mechanism |
| `garelli2013` | Garelli, Espinosa & Gürtler (2013) | Clorinda; household surveys | Clustering and temporal instability | Spatial vector patterns need not be stable | Local immature-stage measure | context |
| `gimenez2020` | Gimenez et al. (2020) | Resistencia; weekly ovitrap study | Minimum temperature and oviposition | Seasonal vector response differs by locality | Does not identify causal dengue burden | mechanism |
| `gurevitz2021` | Gurevitz et al. (2021) | Buenos Aires; georeferenced outbreak analysis | Temperature, travel, housing, poverty | Multiple processes can jointly structure local dengue patterns | One city and outbreak | alternative explanation |
| `carbajo2018` | Carbajo et al. (2018) | Argentina; spatiotemporal modelling | Climate, population, bordering outbreaks | Climate and introductions may jointly matter | Historical model; not this dataset | context |
| `lopez2023` | López et al. (2023) | Argentina; national ecological analysis | Climate and dengue expansion | Long-term climate hypotheses are external context only | Uses different data and outcome definitions | context |
| `flichman2022` | Flichman et al. (2022) | Argentina; blood donors | Seroprevalence and serotypes | Host immunity and serotypes are relevant latent processes | Donors are not the surveillance population | alternative explanation |
| `macchia2025` | Macchia et al. (2025) | Buenos Aires; probability survey | Antibody seroprevalence | Population immunity is measurable independently | City and adult population only | alternative explanation |
| `biscayart2025` | Biscayart et al. (2025) | Buenos Aires; probability survey | Antibody prevalence and inequality | Prior exposure can vary geographically | Not a measure of the current scopes | alternative explanation |
| `mordecai2019` | Mordecai et al. (2019) | Multi-pathogen review | Nonlinear thermal biology | Temperature can affect several transmission traits | General synthesis; no Argentina-specific estimate | mechanism |
| `lambrechts2011` | Lambrechts et al. (2011) | Laboratory experiment | Daily thermal range and DENV transmission | Mean temperature alone is insufficient | Controlled experimental conditions | mechanism |
| `carrington2013life` | Carrington et al. (2013) | Laboratory experiment | Diurnal range and life history | Thermal variability can affect survival and reproduction | Not field transmission | mechanism |
| `carrington2013extreme` | Carrington et al. (2013) | Laboratory experiment and model | Critical thermal extremes | Constant-temperature assumptions can mislead | Experimental parameter range | mechanism |
| `runge2014` | Runge-Ranzinger et al. (2014) | Systematic review | Dengue surveillance performance | Published records need an observation-process interpretation | Heterogeneous systems and eras | surveillance/measurement |
| `stoddard2013` | Stoddard et al. (2013) | Iquitos; human movement study | Household movement | Mobility can couple exposure locations | Different urban setting | alternative explanation |
| `lessani2024` | Lessani et al. (2024) | Systematic review | Mobility and infectious disease | Mobility is a cross-scale explanatory process | Not dengue-specific throughout | context |
| `susong2026` | Susong et al. (2026) | Americas; reporting-delay analysis | Delays and backfilling | Surveillance counts can vary with reporting dynamics | Not an Argentina-specific correction | surveillance/measurement |
| `torres2017` | Torres et al. (2017) | Latin America and Caribbean; systematic review | Epidemiological heterogeneity | Climate and social relations are complex | Broad regional synthesis | context |
| `argentina2025` | Argentine Ministry of Health (2025) | Argentina; official guidance | Detection, notification, classification | Observation-process stages are institutionally specified | Guidance is not an ascertainment estimate | surveillance/measurement |
| `paho2023` | PAHO (2023) | Regional; official guidance | Case definitions and phases | Classification is a distinct observation stage | Not a study of the project data | surveillance/measurement |
