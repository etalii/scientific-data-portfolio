# Implementation comparison policy

| Spec field / exact revision | Implementation locator | Policy | Evidence / reviewer disposition |
|---|---|---|---|

Policies: literal (required exact bytes/string), semantic (meaning preserved,
requires explicit human rationale), editorial (realization may differ within
specified scope/caveats). Record the policy in an existing specification note
and the outcome in review checks. A successful hash check is identity evidence,
not semantic equivalence. No new ontology or automatic language equivalence.
